🎮 GPU - Graphics Processing Unit

The GPU, or Graphics Processing Unit, is a specialized processor designed to accelerate graphics rendering. Originally created for gaming and 3D graphics, GPUs are now essential for video editing, AI workloads, scientific computing, and cryptocurrency mining.

Unlike CPUs with a few powerful cores, GPUs have thousands of smaller cores that excel at parallel processing. This makes them ideal for tasks that can be broken down into many smaller operations, like rendering pixels or training neural networks.

Integrated Graphics

Purpose: Basic display, office work, light media

Integrated graphics are built into the CPU. They share system RAM and are suitable for basic computing, web browsing, office work, and light video playback. They're power-efficient and reduce system cost.
