💻 Laptop Computers

Laptops are portable computers that combine a display, keyboard, trackpad, and internal components in a single, foldable unit. They're designed for mobility, allowing you to work, study, and play from anywhere. Laptops balance performance with portability and battery life.

Purpose: Note-taking, research, study

Student laptops are designed for portability, affordability, and battery life. They handle web browsing, document editing, online research, and educational software with ease.
