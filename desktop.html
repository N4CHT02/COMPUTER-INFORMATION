🖥️ Desktop Computers

Desktop computers are traditional, non-portable computers designed to stay in one location. They offer better performance, upgradeability, and cooling than laptops for the same price. Desktops are ideal for gaming, content creation, professional work, and home use.

Unlike laptops, desktops can be fully customized with interchangeable components, making them more powerful and longer-lasting. They come in various sizes and performance levels to suit different needs and budgets.

Basic Desktop

Purpose: Simple computing, internet browsing, light tasks

Basic desktops are affordable systems for everyday tasks like web browsing, email, and document editing. They're perfect for home use, light office work, and users who don't need high performance.
